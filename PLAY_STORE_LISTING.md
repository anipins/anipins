# Google Play Store listing — AniPins

## Listing copy

App name (7/30): **AniPins**

Short description (70/80):

> Discover, save and explore anime artwork and character collections.

Full description:

> AniPins is an image-first place to discover curated anime fan artwork.
>
> Browse a fresh For You feed, explore what is trending, and search by character, anime series, tags or artwork title. Open any artwork in a high-resolution viewer with pinch, pan and double-tap zoom.
>
> Sign in with the same AniPins account you use on the website. Your likes, saves and collections stay connected across devices. Download artwork through Android's Download Manager or share an AniPins link using the Android share sheet.
>
> Features include:
> - Fresh and trending artwork feeds
> - Anime and character discovery
> - Fast, debounced search
> - Native high-resolution artwork viewer
> - Likes, saves and synchronized collections
> - Native downloads and sharing
> - Light-on-data thumbnail caching and clear offline/error states
> - Secure persistent sign-in and account deletion controls
>
> AniPins is an independent artwork-curation service. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by anime publishers, studios or franchise owners. Artwork and characters remain the property of their respective creators and rights holders. A copyright/takedown process is available in the app and on the website.
