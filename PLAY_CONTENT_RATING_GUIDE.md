# Play content rating / target audience guide

AniPins should not be declared a children's app. It is an independent anime fan-art discovery service with account features, external links and downloadable images. Select the actual intended audience in Play Console; a teen-and-older audience is the prudent default unless the complete catalog is separately reviewed.

Current feature facts for the IARC questionnaire:

- No chat, direct messaging, gambling, purchases, ads or location sharing.
- Ordinary users cannot publicly upload artwork; publishing is administrator-only.
- Users can create profiles, likes, saves, collections and follows.
- The app accesses changing online content and provides links to original creator sources where supplied.
- Artwork is admin curated but may contain stylized fantasy violence, weapons, revealing clothing or suggestive poses depending on the actual catalog.
- A report flow and copyright/takedown process exist.

Before answering, audit every published artwork—especially Featured, Trending and screenshots—for nudity/sexual content, graphic violence, hate symbols, self-harm and illegal content. Remove or age-restrict policy-sensitive content. Answer the live IARC questions truthfully from that audit; do not copy a guessed rating from this document.
